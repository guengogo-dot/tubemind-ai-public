TubeMind AI Privacy Policy

TubeMind AI is a private content workflow tool operated by its owner.

The application uses official platform APIs only after the account owner grants access. For Pinterest, it may access the owner's account and create approved Pins on the owner's behalf.

TubeMind AI does not sell personal data, access other users' private data, send unsolicited messages, or perform uncontrolled bulk actions.

Credentials, access tokens, and app passwords are kept outside the public website and used only by the local application process. The owner can revoke access at any time through Pinterest settings.

For questions, contact the project owner through the project repository.
