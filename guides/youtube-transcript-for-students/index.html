YouTube Transcripts for Students and Research

Use video as a source you can navigate. TubeMind AI makes available YouTube captions searchable, keeps timestamps attached to the original video, and helps you move useful passages into your own study or research workflow.

TubeMind AI workflow

Search available YouTube captions, jump to exact timestamps, copy transcript text or timecodes, and create optional AI summaries without leaving the watch page.

Find the part you need

Search a lecture or interview transcript for a term, person, concept, or phrase. Select the result to return to the exact point in the video and verify the surrounding context.

Build better notes

Copy transcript text for personal notes or keep timestamps when you need to return to the source. The transcript is a navigation aid, not a substitute for checking context.

Optional AI summary

If you configure a compatible AI provider, TubeMind AI can create an optional summary. For academic or research use, verify important claims against the original video and other appropriate sources.

Important limitation

TubeMind AI depends on captions being available for the YouTube video. Users remain responsible for appropriate citation, copyright compliance, and checking source accuracy.
