YouTube Transcript Chrome Extension

Work with a YouTube transcript beside the video instead of constantly switching tabs. TubeMind AI brings available captions into a searchable sidebar with timestamp navigation, copying, timecodes, and optional AI summaries.

TubeMind AI workflow

Search available YouTube captions, jump to exact timestamps, copy transcript text or timecodes, and create optional AI summaries without leaving the watch page.

What a transcript extension can save you

Long lectures, interviews, tutorials, and podcasts are easier to navigate when the spoken content becomes searchable text. Find a phrase first, then jump back to the exact moment for context.

How TubeMind AI works

Open a public YouTube video with captions, let TubeMind AI load the transcript, search or select a segment, and use the timestamp to move the video. Copy and timecode actions are available from the same interface.

Built for focused research

Students, researchers, creators, and anyone reviewing long videos can locate evidence or ideas without repeatedly scrubbing the timeline.

Important limitation

TubeMind AI works with captions that are available for the video. AI summaries are optional and require user configuration of a compatible provider.
