How to Search Inside a YouTube Video

A long video does not need to be a long hunt. When captions are available, TubeMind AI turns them into searchable text so you can locate a topic, name, term, or quotation and jump to its timestamp.

TubeMind AI workflow

Search available YouTube captions, jump to exact timestamps, copy transcript text or timecodes, and create optional AI summaries without leaving the watch page.

Search the words, not the timeline

Load the transcript, enter the word or phrase you remember, and review the matching transcript segments instead of dragging the YouTube playhead back and forth.

Jump to the original moment

Selecting a transcript segment takes you back to the corresponding point in the video, where you can hear the surrounding context.

Useful for long-form video

This workflow is particularly useful for lectures, interviews, podcasts, tutorials, conference talks, and other videos where a useful detail may be buried deep in the runtime.

Important limitation

Search works on the transcript generated from captions available for the video. If captions are unavailable, transcript search cannot provide that text.
