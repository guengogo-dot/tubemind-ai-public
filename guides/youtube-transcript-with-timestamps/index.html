YouTube Transcript With Timestamps

A timestamped transcript turns a long video into navigable text. TubeMind AI can copy transcript content with timecodes so notes keep their connection to the original video.

What TubeMind AI does

TubeMind AI is a Chrome extension for working with available YouTube captions: transcript search, timestamp navigation, active transcript highlighting, copying, timecodes, and optional AI summaries.

How to do it

  1. Load a captioned YouTube video.
  2. Open the transcript in TubeMind AI.
  3. Enable timecodes when copying.
  4. Paste the transcript into your notes and keep the timestamps for reference.

Why this is useful

Instead of repeatedly seeking through a long video, you can work with its text, locate the section you need, and return to the original timestamp when context matters.

Important limitation

Transcript features depend on captions being available for the YouTube video. AI summaries are optional and require user configuration of a compatible provider.

Open TubeMind AI in the Chrome Web Store
